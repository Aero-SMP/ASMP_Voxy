Scope: approach1 only. Feature branch feature/cache-first-background-updates.
Renderer/GPU reports, raw key/area ordering, revision checks, retention API/calls,
storage/disk-exhaustion protection, publication protocol and worker count remain unchanged.

Implementation
VisibleSectionState is coordinator-owned. A primitive key/index map plus dense key/
seen-epoch arrays detects unique additions and removals from complete reports.
Ancestor and region reference counts emit only zero/nonzero membership transitions.
All additions precede removals. Reordered/duplicate keys do not change membership.
Raw report epochs and key/area arrays still advance when membership is unchanged.

WorldCacheDownloads consumes guarded membership deltas. Helper identity, current
Dimension identity and base generation must match; otherwise it copies current
membership. HELLO delays, skipped owner deltas, new helpers, detach/reconnect and
manifest/dimension replacement resync without retaining history. Existing directory
draining, lifecycle retention clears, cancellation before expansion and required
frontier rebuilds remain.

Reports are inspected in O(incoming entries + previous visible keys). Ordinary
derived work is expected O(delta * at-most-five ancestor levels). No-new-report
membership checks are O(1). Frontier/job/coverage and budget costs remain separate.
A >=512-key shrink leaving <=25% survivors rebuilds references from survivors and
trims the original maps once to avoid repeated per-removal rehashing.

Memory
Primitive state and reusable delta arrays are bounded by peak keys/watches/regions
and deltas; arrays may retain peak capacity after shrink. Fewer allocated bytes do
not establish reduced retained heap. Total memory and full-report inspection are
not O(1).

Validation
Independent full-rebuild oracle: exact memberships and transition sets, duplicate
zero, coordinate boundaries, shared ancestors/regions, parent-child replacement,
stale/skipped epochs, 3000 random reports, shrink/grow cycles and read-only views.
Real planner: first sync, deltas, missed generation/empty delta, helper identity,
detach/reconnect, dimension/manifest change, unknown dimension, anchor movement,
shared retention clearing, job cancellation/drop acknowledgment, processing/other
dimension preservation, added-root expansion and exact frontier comparator order.
Existing normal/debug shutdown, callback lock-order, admission, allocator/topology
and stale-fence fixtures passed. Normal/debug JARs exclude offline test classes.
